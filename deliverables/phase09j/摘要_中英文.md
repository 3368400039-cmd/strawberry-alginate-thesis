# 摘 要

为比较不同双步浸渍流程在完整草莓短期常温贮藏中的品质表现，本研究设置水处理CK、氯化钙处理Ca和海藻酸钠—氯化钙处理SA-Ca，开展三个日期批次的四天观察。连续观察队列记录失重及累计腐烂，破坏性取样队列测定表观硬度和可溶性固形物（TSS），以容器为主要层级进行描述统计。

三组失重率均随贮藏日升高。终点SA-Ca失重率为7.725%，高于CK的7.315%，但低于Ca；同期Ca累计腐烂比例最低，SA-Ca可测果的表观硬度均值最高，CK的TSS均值最高。TSS由起点至第2天均升高，此后Ca均值不变，CK和SA-Ca略升。部分组间差在不同日期批次中的方向不一致。

四项指标反映不同品质侧面，单项较好表现未同时对应其他指标的统一优势。由于未建立综合权重与接受阈值，本研究不确定总体最优处理。结果为给定流程下的多指标比较提供了短期观察依据，也表明评价时需要同时考虑对照、取样层级及批次背景。后续研究应围绕具体品质目标预先确定评价终点，再检验处理的适用条件。

关键词：草莓; 海藻酸钠; 双步浸渍; 常温贮藏; 品质评价

# Abstract

This study compared three two-step dipping procedures for whole strawberries during short-term ambient storage: water treatment (CK), calcium chloride treatment (Ca), and sodium alginate followed by calcium chloride treatment (SA-Ca). Three date batches were observed for four days. A continuous-observation cohort was used for mass loss and cumulative decay, while different fruits were sampled destructively for apparent firmness and total soluble solids (TSS). Descriptive statistics were calculated primarily at the container level.

Mean mass loss increased in all groups. Final mean mass loss in SA-Ca was 7.725%, higher than the 7.315% recorded for CK but lower than that in Ca. Ca had the lowest final cumulative decay proportion, SA-Ca had the highest final mean apparent firmness among measurable fruits, and CK had the highest final mean TSS. TSS increased from the initial sampling to day 2; thereafter, the Ca group mean remained unchanged, while CK and SA-Ca increased slightly. Some between-group contrasts changed direction across date batches.

The four indicators represented different aspects of quality, and favorable performance in one indicator did not coincide with consistent advantages in the others. No overall best treatment was identified because a composite weighting scheme and acceptance thresholds had not been established. The findings provide short-term observations for comparing the specified procedures and highlight the relevance of controls, sampling levels, and batch backgrounds. Further studies should define endpoints for specific quality goals before testing treatment applicability.

Keywords: strawberry; sodium alginate; two-step dipping; ambient storage; quality evaluation
